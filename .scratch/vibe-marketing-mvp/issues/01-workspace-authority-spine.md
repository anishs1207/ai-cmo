# 01 — Create the workspace and authority spine

**What to build:** A persistent workspace for one business with Owner, Admin, and Viewer authority, tenant isolation, and immutable audit events for material actions.

**Blocked by:** None — can start immediately.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Implemented in commit:** —

- [ ] A user can create and revisit a persistent business workspace.
- [ ] Admin and Viewer permissions are enforced on every workspace access path.
- [ ] Material actions record the actor, workspace, action, and time in an append-only audit history.
