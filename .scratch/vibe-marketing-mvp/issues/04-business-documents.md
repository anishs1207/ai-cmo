# 04 — Import supported business documents

**What to build:** An operator can add text-based PDF, DOCX, and TXT documents as business sources and understand when a document cannot be processed.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Complete manual business intake.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Implemented in commit:** —

- [ ] Text is extracted within published size and page limits.
- [ ] Image-only or scanned documents are detected and rejected with an OCR-unavailable explanation.
- [ ] Embedded images are ignored and the extracted source remains traceable.
