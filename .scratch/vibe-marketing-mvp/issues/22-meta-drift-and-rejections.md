# 22 — Reconcile external Meta changes and rejected ads

**What to build:** The system safely incorporates direct Meta edits and guides compliant revision of rejected ads without overwriting users or evading policy.

**Blocked by:** 17 — Publish and launch an approved Meta campaign; 20 — Approve and execute post-launch actions.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Implemented in commit:** —

- [ ] External changes are imported, audited, and never automatically overwritten.
- [ ] Stale recommendations are invalidated and the user chooses managed or monitoring-only status.
- [ ] Rejection reasons, affected material, revisions, and version history are preserved without automatic resubmission loops.
