One important design choice

I would avoid hardcoding "Meta Ads" into the core of the platform. Instead, make the optimization engine channel-agnostic.

For example:

Core AI Marketing Brain
        │
 ┌──────┼──────┐
 │      │      │
Meta   Google  TikTok
 │      │      │
Pinterest LinkedIn X

The AI decides what strategy to execute, while adapters for each platform handle how to execute it. That way, adding support for Google Ads or TikTok Ads later is mostly a matter of implementing another connector rather than redesigning the entire system.

If you execute this well, you're not competing with individual tools like ad builders or UGC generators. You're competing with the workflow of a small performance marketing agency by automating the repetitive, data-driven parts of campaign creation, testing, optimization, and reporting. That broader scope is likely to create more defensible value than focusing on any single capability.
