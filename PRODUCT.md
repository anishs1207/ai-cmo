# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Early-stage founders. They're wearing marketing, product, and ops at once — often alone or with a tiny team. They open Ripple at a desk during work hours to get organic growth running without hiring an agency, then come back to see whether posts actually sent traffic.

## Product Purpose

Ripple is an organic growth autopilot: it learns the business from the website, writes platform-native posts (starting with Reddit), publishes with tracked links, and shows whether campaigns actually moved visitors. Success is a founder who trusts the agent enough to ship campaigns and can see, in one place, how well they did.

Marketing surfaces exist to create that trust before sign-up. Product surfaces exist to execute and measure without noise.

## Brand Personality

**calm · craft · confident**

Quiet competence over hype. The interface should feel like a well-made tool in a workshop — olive, ink, and near-black structure inspired by [openhuman](https://github.com/icyzh/openhuman) — not like a loud AI launch page. Voice is plain, specific, and founder-to-founder.

## Anti-references

- Purple-on-white / indigo gradient “AI SaaS” chrome
- Generic Apple HIG cool-gray + system blue product shells (what Ripple looks like today)
- Warm cream / sand / terracotta editorial AI defaults
- Identical icon + heading + blurb card grids and tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section
- Decorative glassmorphism, glow effects, and bounce-heavy motion on everyday product actions

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before spectacle** — Landing earns the click; the app earns the return visit. Never let marketing motion leak into daily product flow.
2. **One job per surface** — Landing sells the autopilot; dashboard answers “how did we do?”; campaigns are the workbench; KB is the source of truth.
3. **Show the loop** — Scrape → know → post → track → learn. Make that cycle legible without a feature tour.
4. **Quiet materials, clear actions** — Olive canvas and dark structure for atmosphere; primary actions stay obvious and sparse.
5. **Feedback over decoration** — Press states, toasts, and status chips exist so founders feel the system heard them; no animation for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (opacity/color only; no large travel animations). Touch targets ≥44px where primary. No information by color alone — pair status color with label/chip text.
